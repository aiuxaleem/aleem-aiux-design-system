# LinkedIn caption: Figma Weave, visual node-based AI workflows

Figma Weave turns generation into a pipeline you can see. A node graph sits beside your frames, so every model, every step and every alternative lives on one canvas.

Four ideas worth knowing:

1. Sculpting multi-model pipelines. Connect multiple AI models together to sequentially transform assets and sculpt outputs like clay: text-to-image, image-to-image style transfer, upscale and refine, then the final output lands as a frame.

2. Beyond single-prompt generative AI. Weave replaces prompt trial-and-error with a visual node workspace alongside Figma frames.

3. Inspect, compare, and iterate. Build pipelines to evaluate alternative generation approaches and inspect intermediate canvas outputs.

4. Reusable creative tools and templates. Save and share custom node pipelines as reusable team templates and community tools.
