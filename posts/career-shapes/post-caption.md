# LinkedIn caption: Career shapes

Which shape is your expertise?

Four letters describe how deep and how broad your knowledge goes.

I-shaped: deep in one area and little else. Most careers start here.
T-shaped: deep in one discipline, conversant across many others.
V-shaped: deep in one area, widening into the fields next to it.
M-shaped: deep in two or three areas, with a shared layer linking them.

The shape is not a ranking. It is a map of where you can go next: a T adds breadth, a V adds neighbours, an M adds a second depth.

Which letter are you today, and which one are you growing into?
